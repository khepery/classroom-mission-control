1. DATA LAB BACKDROPS  (painted rooms that sit behind the moving apparatus)
============================================================
The app draws the apparatus, labels and dials itself and darkens the picture, so keep these calm, dark, low detail, with an empty middle and a flat surface along the bottom fifth. Save these in a folder called lab next to index.html.
STYLE: painted retro sci-fi illustration, wide 1600x1000, dark navy and teal palette, soft lighting, blurred and out of focus like a photo background, large empty calm area in the centre, a plain workbench or ground along the bottom, no people, no text, no numbers, no apparatus in the middle

[bg-phys] a physics lab inside a space station, metal walls, a round porthole window with stars on the left, shelves with faint dials and meters at the edges.
[bg-chem] a chemistry lab on a spaceship, a fume cupboard and glassware shelves blurred at the sides, warm amber lamps.
[bg-bio] a biology lab greenhouse on a space station, potted plants and microscopes blurred at the edges, soft green light.
[bg-street] a small market street at dusk on a moon colony, shop fronts and glowing signs blurred at the sides, empty pavement in the middle.
[bg-valley] a wide open valley at dawn under a big sky with faint stars, distant hills only, nothing tall in the middle.
[bg-games] a retro games room on a starship, a felt table along the bottom, soft neon glow at the edges.
