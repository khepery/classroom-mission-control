Photo → Editable Text

Turn a photo of a textbook page, worksheet or board into text you can edit, then reuse it as a word list, gap-fill, dictation or slides.

Private by design All recognition happens on this device. Images and text are never uploaded. PDF files are not supported: take a photo or a screenshot of the page instead.
