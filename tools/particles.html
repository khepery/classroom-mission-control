Particle Sandbox

Paint playful materials and discover surprising patterns. Everything stays on this device.

Loading local Sandspiel engine…

Drag to paint. Keyboard: focus the canvas, use arrow keys to move the brush, and Space or Enter to paint. Eraser removes materials. Pause lets you draw a still scene.

Try a challenge

  • Build a wall with a gap. Can you guide sand into a small container?
  • Make a pond, plant some seeds, then predict what will happen.
  • Compare ice, water and lava. Pause, predict, then resume!

A creative toy, not accurate chemistry or a scientific model. Its rules are playful; do not use it to predict real-world reactions.
